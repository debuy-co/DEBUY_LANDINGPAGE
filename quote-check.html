Objections, answered

Is my supplier's pricing confidential?

Yes. Offers are processed only to produce your verdict — never shared or used across customers. EU data residency, GDPR-compliant.

What if a line item can't be benchmarked?

The verdict says so explicitly. An honest fallback beats a confident guess — you'll never get an unexplained number.

Do I need to connect my ERP first?

No. Quote Check works on the document alone. Connecting your data later unlocks portfolio-level intelligence.
